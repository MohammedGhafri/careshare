Product concept
CareCircle AI helps older or vulnerable people living independently, together with their families, carers, and trusted volunteers, coordinate everyday support.
The initial target market is England, focused on family-led support for older people living at home.
Core proposition
Create one shared place where a trusted care circle can see:
Medication schedules and reminders
Whether medication was taken, missed, declined, or unknown
Upcoming visits and wellbeing check-ins
Tasks and who is responsible
Important contacts
Recent activity
Alerts requiring attention
Escalations and who is responsible for responding
Key principle
Rules detect → AI explains → humans act.
AI should help identify and explain potential issues but must never make clinical decisions.

Roles
Supported PersonCan see their support plan, medication, visits, contacts, and preferences.
Family CoordinatorManages the care circle, schedules, alerts, and escalation.
CarerViews assigned activities and records visits, medication support, and concerns.
Trusted VolunteerOnly sees the minimum information required for their assigned visits/tasks.
AI behaviour
AI can:
Summarise alerts
Explain why something needs attention
Prioritise outstanding coordination issues
Suggest operational next steps
Summarise recent activity
AI cannot:
Diagnose
Prescribe
Recommend medication doses
Tell someone whether to take or skip medication
Determine whether abuse or neglect has occurred
Automatically contact emergency services
Make autonomous care decisions
Always keep a human in control.
Prototype constraints
This is a prototype to be built in less than one day, so optimise for simplicity and demonstration value.
Use:
Responsive web app
Synthetic/mock data only
Simple role switching rather than complex authentication
Rules-based alerts
AI only for explanations/summaries
Simulated notifications
Simple local/database storage
Clear visual status colours:
Green = completed
Blue = normal
Amber = attention
Red = urgent human attention
Demo scenario
Create a fictional older person called Margaret who lives independently.
Her care circle includes:
Daughter — family coordinator
Carer
Trusted volunteer
Demonstrate this journey:
Margaret onboarded → care circle created → medication added → volunteer visit scheduled → medication status becomes unknown → AI alert generated → daughter acknowledges → unsuccessful contact attempt logged → alert escalates → Margaret contacted successfully → issue resolved → audit trail updated.
UX principles
The interface should feel:
Calm
Reassuring
Simple
Accessible
Human
Trustworthy
Design primarily for older users and busy family members.
Use large text, large touch targets, simple navigation, plain English, strong contrast, and minimal cognitive load.
Safety boundary
Display clearly throughout the product:
CareCircle AI helps coordinate support. It does not provide medical advice or replace professional or emergency services.
The prototype should demonstrate how AI can make informal care coordination more visible, proactive, and connected while keeping people—not AI—in control.
