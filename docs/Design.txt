App Colour Palette
Primary colours
Deep Teal — #0F766E
Trust, stability and care. Use for primary navigation, headers, key buttons and core branding.
Sage Green — #10B981
Health, wellbeing and positivity. Use for positive actions, progress and supportive care elements.
Soft Mint — #D1FAE5
Calm, friendly and open. Use for backgrounds, cards, selected states and subtle highlights.
Secondary colours
Navy — #1E293B
Structure and readability. Use for strong headings, navigation and high-emphasis content.
Cool Grey — #F1F5F9
Clean, modern and neutral. Use for page backgrounds, cards and inactive UI areas.
Warm Neutral — #FFF7ED
Warmth and approachability. Use for alternative backgrounds, family-focused areas and softer content sections.
Accent colours
Blush Pink — #F472B6
Compassion and human connection. Use sparingly for caring/family moments, highlights and brand accents.
Amber — #F59E0B
Attention and important updates. Use for reminders, things needing attention and upcoming actions.
Sky Blue — #60A5FA
Information and communication. Use for informational content, appointments, links and secondary actions.
Lilac — #A78BFA
Community and support. Use for family/carer coordination, people and shared-care elements.
Status colours
Success — #22C55E
Use for completed medication, completed visits, confirmed actions and on-track care.
Warning — #F59E0B
Use for medication due, upcoming actions or something requiring attention.
Error / Urgent — #EF4444
Use for missed medication, urgent issues, overdue actions or critical alerts.
Information — #3B82F6
Use for general information, updates and informational notices.
Text & UI colours
Primary Text — #111827
Use for headings and important text.
Secondary Text — #6B7280
Use for body copy, supporting information, timestamps and captions.
Borders / Dividers — #E5E7EB
Use for card borders, input fields, separators and dividers.
Quick design principle
Teal + mint should dominate the experience, creating a calm, trustworthy feel. Use pink, blue and lilac to distinguish people and supportive information, while green, amber and red should have consistent functional meanings for care-plan statuses and alerts.
